Potato Soup



Description

I prefer to cook from scratch, but this 5 can potato soup is my go-to when I want comfort without the effort. Creamy, cheesy, and satisfying, it delivers all the flavor of a loaded baked potato with almost zero prep. Garnish with chives, bacon, and more Cheddar if you want to take it over the top.

Ingredients

  • 1 (14.5 ounce) can chicken broth
  • 1 (12 ounce) can evaporated milk
  • 1 (10.5 ounce) can condensed Cheddar cheese soup
  • 1 (10.5 ounce) can condensed cream of chicken soup
  • 1 (15 ounce) can diced potatoes, drained
  • chives, crumbled bacon, shredded Cheddar cheese, for garnish (optional)

Steps

  1. Whisk chicken broth, evaporated milk, Cheddar cheese soup, and cream of chicken soup together in a saucepan over medium heat until well blended. Add diced potatoes; cook until hot, 5 to 10 minutes.
